# Venue information

The conference takes place in the first-floor Ballroom at Wynn Macau. Follow the Ballroom signs inside the hotel.

- Address: Rua Cidade de Sintra, NAPE, Macau
- Hotel telephone: +853 2888 9966
- Complimentary shuttle stop: North Entrance, outside Wynn Esplanade

- Registration desk: Meeting Foyer
- Main venue and November 24 banquet: Ballroom 1
- Breakout rooms: Meeting Room 1 and Meeting Room 2
- Lunch: Ballrooms 2, 3 and 4
- Art Gallery: November 23–24, Art Gallery Area in the Meeting Foyer
