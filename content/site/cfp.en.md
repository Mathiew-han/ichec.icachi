# ICHEC 2026 Author Notes

The Call for Papers page is organized by submission track. Please first choose the track that matches your work, then follow the corresponding topics, timeline, and material requirements listed above.

## General Scope

ICHEC 2026 welcomes work across the full spectrum of Human-Computer Interaction and Human-Engaged Computing, including but not limited to:

- Human-AI collaboration, generative AI and HCI, intelligent interaction, and responsible technology
- Accessibility, aging, health, education, families, games, play, and social computing
- Interaction techniques, devices, modalities, engineering interactive systems, tools, and visualization
- Design, user experience, usability, digital heritage, interactive narrative, and visual storytelling
- Privacy, security, ethics, critical computing, cross-cultural interaction, theory, concepts, and methods

Submissions focusing on Macao characteristics, local innovation, regional cultural features, and inclusive international HCI perspectives are especially encouraged.

## Shared Submission Policies

- **Language**: ICHEC 2026 accepts English submissions only.
- **Submission portal**: Papers and artworks should be submitted through [EasyChair](https://easychair.org/my/conference?conf=ichec2026). Workshop participants should review the [accepted workshop information](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mtPgChHjtDAH_UauceDvsBAUSr275UEztXoQs9jU51I/edit?usp=sharing) and use the registration link for their chosen workshop.
- **Originality**: Submissions must not have been previously published and must not be under concurrent review by another conference or journal.
- **Anonymity**: Tracks that use blinded review require authors to remove identifying information from the paper and supplementary materials.
- **Formatting**: Paper submissions should use the single-column review format specified in the official submission instructions.
- **Registration**: At least one author or contributor of each accepted paper or artwork is expected to register and attend to present or exhibit. All workshop organizers and participants must register for both the workshop and the main conference; no additional workshop fee applies.


## Generative AI and Academic Integrity

Authors are responsible for all submitted content. If generative AI tools are used for writing, polishing, analysis support, or other purposes beyond ordinary editing, authors should disclose the use appropriately. Generative AI tools should not be listed as authors.

## Workshop Participation

Four workshops have been accepted and the call for participants is now open. Participants must register for both their chosen workshop and the main conference; no additional workshop fee applies. Room assignments and exact session times will be announced later. See the accepted Workshop section above for posters, descriptions, registration links, and organiser contacts.
