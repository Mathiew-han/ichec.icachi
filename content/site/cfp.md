# ICHEC 2026 Call for Participation Notes

This page is organized by submission track. Please use the track sections above for the specific topics, dates, and required materials that apply to your submission.

## Shared Policies

- **Submission portal**: Papers and artworks should be submitted through [EasyChair](https://easychair.org/my/conference?conf=ichec2026). Workshop participants should review the [accepted workshop information](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mtPgChHjtDAH_UauceDvsBAUSr275UEztXoQs9jU51I/edit?usp=sharing) and use the registration link for their chosen workshop.
- ICHEC 2026 accepts English submissions only.
- **Formatting**: Paper submissions should use the single-column review format specified in the official submission instructions.
- Tracks using blinded review require author-identifying information to be removed from papers and supplementary materials.
- Four workshops have been accepted and the call for participants is open. Register for both the chosen workshop and the main conference; no additional workshop fee applies.
